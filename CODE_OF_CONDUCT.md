# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GameShelf to be a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free space for everyone who takes part, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, or beliefs. We pledge to act in ways that keep the community open, friendly and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code or the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming otherwise.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful.
- Credit others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or spam (including self-promotion unrelated to the project).
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as an address or email) without permission.
- Sustained pressure on maintainers or other contributors, such as repeated demands for reviews or merges.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, comments and code reviews, and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. The maintainer may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

### Reporting

To report a violation, contact the maintainer privately at **hardikmalhotra150804@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled with discretion, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

### Possible responses

Depending on severity, the maintainer may:

1. Give a private warning and a request to change the behavior.
2. Give a public warning.
3. Impose a temporary ban from the project's spaces.
4. Impose a permanent ban.
